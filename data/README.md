# Data stays local

Download and keep your own AQICN export here. The pipeline accepts an Excel or CSV file with `date` and `pm25` columns; other pollutant columns are ignored in this first experiment.

Selected period: 2021-07-01 through 2026-07-01, both dates included.
Source: https://aqicn.org/historical/cn/#city:chengdu

The downloaded values are PM2.5-specific AQI, not raw concentration. The export alone does not establish the precise station or the daily aggregation method. Do not label it station-specific daily mean concentration.

AQICN's published terms restrict redistribution of archived data. Keep raw and derived data out of this public repository. Attribute WAQI and the originating environmental agency when identified. The data are not fully verified by WAQI. See the source page for current terms.
